# Indian Income Tax Act — TDS Regulations for Payment Aggregators & FinOps

## Section 194-O: Payment of certain sums by E-Commerce Operator to E-Commerce Participants
- **Statutory Rate:** 1.00% (0.01) on the gross amount of sales or services.
- **Scope:** Any payment facilitated by an e-commerce operator to an e-commerce participant (merchant/seller) for sale of goods or provision of services through its digital or electronic facility/platform.
- **Threshold Limit:** TDS is not required if the gross amount of sales/services does not exceed ₹5,00,000 in a financial year, provided the participant is an Individual or HUF and furnishes PAN/Aadhaar.
- **Non-PAN Rate:** If the deductee does not furnish a valid PAN, the rate of deduction under Section 206AA escalates to 5.00%.
- **FinOps Guideline:** Payment aggregators and marketplace platforms must compute 1% TDS on the gross transaction value prior to deducting platform fees or gateway commission.

## Section 194H: Commission or Brokerage
- **Statutory Rate:** 5.00% (0.05) on the commission or brokerage amount.
- **Scope:** Any person (not being an individual or HUF who is not liable to tax audit) paying any income by way of commission or brokerage to a resident. Includes merchant acquiring commission, merchant discount rate (MDR) revenue shares, and referral broker fees.
- **Threshold Limit:** No deduction if the aggregate amount credited or paid during the financial year does not exceed ₹15,000.
- **Non-PAN Rate:** 20.00% under Section 206AA if PAN is not furnished.
- **FinOps Guideline:** Any brokerage or intermediary commission paid to third-party channel partners or ISOs must be deducted at 5%.

## Section 194C: Payments to Contractors and Sub-Contractors
- **Statutory Rate:**
  - 1.00% (0.01) where the payment is made to an Individual or Hindu Undivided Family (HUF).
  - 2.00% (0.02) where the payment is made to any other entity (Company, Firm, LLP, AOP, BOI).
- **Scope:** Any sum paid for carrying out any work (including transport, logistics, courier operations, security, catering, and facility management).
- **Threshold Limit:**
  - Single contract payment exceeds ₹30,000, OR
  - Aggregate payments during the financial year exceed ₹1,00,000.
- **FinOps Guideline:** Delivery partner disbursements, delivery fleet vendor payments, and logistics vendor billings are governed under Section 194C (2% for corporate entities).

---

## Section 194J: Fees for Professional or Technical Services
- **Statutory Rate:**
  - 10.00% (0.10) for Professional Services (legal, medical, engineering, architecture, accountancy, technical consultancy, interior decoration, advertising).
  - 2.00% (0.02) for Technical Services (fees for technical services FTS, royalty in the nature of software considerations, call center operations).
- **Threshold Limit:** ₹30,000 per financial year per category of service.
- **FinOps Guideline:** Software engineering vendors, core banking API integration consultants, and legal audit retainers fall under Section 194J.
